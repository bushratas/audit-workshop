Backup: longer sentences

Use these if Translate shows you both a masculine and a feminine version. Longer sentences usually force it to pick one. Watch the last one: it pulls in two directions at once.

Bengali pronouns are not gendered. The pronoun ও covers he, she and they alike, so none of these sentences say whether the person is a man or a woman. Any gender in the English came from the system, not the original.
